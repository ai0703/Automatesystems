# AutomateSystems — Master Offer Documentation (Sales → Delivery → Scale)

## 1. Agency Overview

**AutomateSystems** is an AI-automation agency that builds *done-for-you* infrastructure to remove manual work from **recruiting** and **sales/lead generation** processes—so clients can increase throughput while reducing costs. The mission is to help businesses integrate AI into their sales and recruiting systems to accelerate growth and lower operational load.

AutomateSystems is positioned as **“the infrastructure behind high-performing sales and recruitment teams.”** The client experience stays simple; the complexity stays internal.

---

## 2. Target Audience (ICP)

AutomateSystems primarily serves mid-sized B2B companies with clear demand but bottlenecks in either **candidate flow** or **lead flow**.

### Core segments

1) **Staffing & recruitment agencies**
- €5M–€50M revenue
- ~10–50 active roles per month
- Recruiter team already in place
- Bottleneck exists in sourcing + screening (not sales)

2) **Lead-generation & marketing agencies**
- Running outbound/appointment-setting campaigns for clients
- Need a scalable delivery engine without hiring more SDRs

3) **Service-based B2B businesses**
- Consultancies, SaaS firms, agencies
- Existing sales team
- Need consistent pipeline of qualified leads/meetings

### Not a fit
- Early-stage companies without clear ICP, role briefs, or an operational baseline
- Companies that cannot provide feedback quickly (within 48 hours)

---

## 3. Core Problems We Solve

AutomateSystems solves two high-cost bottlenecks:

### A) Candidate flow bottleneck (recruitment)
- Roles stack faster than candidates enter pipeline
- Recruiters spend disproportionate time sourcing/screening
- Time-to-first-interview is too slow
- Burnout rises; fill rates drop

### B) Lead flow + appointment-setting bottleneck (sales)
- Manual prospecting and outreach wastes time
- Inconsistent follow-ups reduce conversion
- Cost-per-appointment is too high
- Outreach only happens in “business hours” instead of 24/7

# 5. Offer #1 — Candidate Pipeline Accelerator

## 5.1 Offer Overview

**Offer Name:** Candidate Pipeline Accelerator  
**Offer Type:** Monthly candidate delivery infrastructure (Done-For-You)  
**Category:** Staffing & Recruitment Enablement

### Core Problem We Solve
Staffing agencies at scale typically do not have a sales problem or a “talent shortage.”

They have a **candidate flow bottleneck**:
- Roles stack faster than qualified candidates enter pipeline
- Recruiters spend most time sourcing/screening
- Time-to-first-interview becomes too slow

The Candidate Pipeline Accelerator removes sourcing and screening from recruiters and replaces it with a guaranteed candidate delivery system.

---

## 5.2 Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

### Who This Is For
- €5M–€50M staffing agencies
- 10–50 active roles per month
- Existing recruiter team in place
- Clear demand from clients (roles already sold)

### Who This Is NOT For
- Agencies under €5M
- <10 roles/month
- Companies without defined role briefs
- Clients unable to interview candidates within 7 days

---

## 5.3 The Core Promise (Locked)

> **For each active role, we deliver 3–5 interview-ready candidates within 7 days.**

### What This Promise Means
- Speed to interview is guaranteed
- Candidates are pre-qualified and aligned
- Recruiters do not source or screen

### What This Promise Does NOT Mean
- No placement guarantees
- No employment warranties
- No unlimited candidates

---

## 5.4 Offer Structure

- Monthly recurring service
- Role-based delivery
- Independent 7-day SLA per role

Each role is treated as its own pipeline with its own clock.

---

## 5.5 Pricing & Tiers

**Tier 1 — Standard Delivery**
- **€1,500 / month**
- Up to **3 active roles**
- **3–5 interview-ready candidates per role**
- **7-day** delivery window per role

**Tier 2 — Scale Delivery**
- **€2,000 / month**
- **4–7 active roles**
- Priority channel allocation
- Parallel role fulfillment

**Pricing logic** is anchored against:
- Recruiter salary cost (€4–6k/month)
- Lost revenue from slow fills
- Opportunity cost of recruiter time

---

## 5.6 What Clients Receive (Client-Facing)

Clients receive:
- Interview-ready candidates only
- Structured candidate summaries
- Fast delivery timelines
- Feedback-driven optimization

Clients do **not** see:
- Sourcing channels
- Outreach volume
- AI tooling
- Internal workflows

**Outcome is the product.**

---

## 5.7 Fulfillment System (High-Level)

Each role moves through:
1. Role normalization
2. Channel allocation
3. Sourcing
4. Outreach
5. Screening
6. Qualification
7. Ranking
8. Client delivery

---

## 5.8 Definition of “Interview‑Ready”

A candidate is only delivered if they:
- Meet **≥80%** of must-have skills
- Have relevant prior experience
- Confirm compensation alignment
- Confirm availability to interview
- Have no known deal-breakers

---

## 5.9 Feedback & Optimization Loop

Clients must:
- Review candidates within **48 hours**
- Provide rejection reasons

Feedback is used to:
- Refine screening
- Adjust sourcing
- Improve future delivery speed

---

## 5.10 SLA & Guarantee Scope

**Guaranteed:** delivery of **3–5 interview-ready candidates** within **7 days per role**.

**Not guaranteed:** interviews conducted, offers made, placements closed.

---

## 5.11 Competitive Differentiation

Unlike traditional models:
- Not pay-per-placement
- Not recruiter outsourcing
- Not job ads or CV databases

This is **candidate flow infrastructure**.

# 6. Offer #2 — AI Lead‑Generation Automation System (Lead Gen Automation)

## 6.1 Offer Overview

**Offer Name:** AI Lead‑Generation Automation System (Lead Gen Automation)  
**Offer Type:** Done‑for‑you lead‑generation + appointment‑setting infrastructure (subscription or project-based)  
**Category:** Sales and Marketing Enablement

### Core Problem We Solve
Traditional lead generation is inefficient and costly:
- High outreach volume for low returns
- Inconsistent follow-ups
- Generic outreach that misses timing and context
- Manual methods that don’t scale

Our Lead Gen Automation system removes prospect research and appointment scheduling from SDRs/teams. It automatically finds high-value prospects, enriches data, crafts personalized outreach, and books meetings so the team can focus on closing.

---

## 6.2 Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

### Who This Is For
- Lead-generation and marketing agencies running outbound for clients
- Service-based businesses with existing sales teams needing consistent qualified meetings
- Mid-sized B2B companies (€5M–€50M) with enough capacity to handle meetings
- Companies with a defined ICP and clear offer

### Who This Is NOT For
- Early-stage startups without a clear ICP or sales process
- Businesses looking for talent recruitment (use Candidate Pipeline Accelerator)
- Companies unwilling to follow up within 48 hours and provide feedback

---

## 6.3 The Core Promise (Locked)

> **Save at least 80% of your appointment-setting costs and fill your calendar with qualified, high-intent leads.**

### What This Promise Means
- We automate at least **80%** of the outreach, qualification, and scheduling workload.
- The client experiences a comparable reduction in manual effort and appointment-setting costs.

### What This Promise Does NOT Mean
- No guarantee of closed deals or revenue outcomes (performance depends on sales execution).

---

## 6.4 Offer Structure

- Monthly subscription (optional implementation fee)
- Scope: lead sourcing, data enrichment, qualification, personalized outreach, appointment scheduling
- SLA: deliver agreed number of qualified leads per week + manage scheduling within client calendar

---

## 6.5 Pricing & Tiers (Illustrative)

| Tier | Monthly fee | Campaigns | Qualified leads / month | Highlights |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Starter** | €1,200 | 1–2 | 20–30 | AI prospecting + enrichment + outreach; appointment scheduling; CRM integration |
| **Growth** | €2,000 | 3–5 | 50–75 | Priority data sources; multichannel outreach (email/LinkedIn/voice); custom sequences |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | 6+ | 100+ | Dedicated AI agents; custom models/integrations; white-label reporting |

Pricing is anchored against the cost of hiring SDRs and manual outbound. Clients can start with a pilot campaign and scale after ROI is proven.

---

## 6.6 What Clients Receive (Client‑Facing)

Clients receive:
- High-intent leads identified via predictive scoring + multiple data sources
- Enriched prospect profiles (firmographics, contact details, context)
- Personalized outreach + follow-ups (multi-channel)
- Appointment scheduling into client calendar
- Performance dashboard (leads, meetings, engagement)

Clients do **not** need to manage prospecting tools or run manual outreach—AutomateSystems runs the system end-to-end.

---

## 6.7 What’s Included (Internal / Operational)

Internally, fulfillment can include:
- Prospecting agents (scrape/identify leads across LinkedIn, directories, websites)
- Qualification agents (intent signals + prioritization)
- Engagement agents (generate/send personalized messages; handle responses)
- Scheduling bots (book/reschedule/confirm meetings)
- CRM + marketing automation integrations (sync status, notes, outcomes)

---

## 6.8 Client Readiness Requirements

No campaign launches without:
- Defined ICP + campaign goals
- Calendar + CRM access for scheduling/sync
- Buy-in to follow up within 48 hours and provide feedback
- Compliance confirmation for relevant data privacy laws (e.g., GDPR/CCPA)

---

## 6.9 Onboarding Process

1) **Commercial onboarding**: sign contract, choose tier, billing setup

2) **Strategy & intake**: define ICP, objectives, messaging guidelines; connect calendars + CRMs

3) **System configuration**: deploy agents, set up connectors, scoring models

4) **Launch & calibration**: start smaller to calibrate scoring + messaging; begin scheduling

5) **Scale & optimize**: increase volume once KPIs are met; refine models based on performance

---

## 6.10 Fulfillment System (High‑Level)

1. Define ICP & campaign strategy
2. Data sourcing & enrichment
3. Lead scoring & qualification
4. Personalized outreach & follow-ups
5. Appointment scheduling
6. Feedback & optimization

---

## 6.11 Definition of a “Qualified Lead”

A lead is delivered only if they:
- Match **≥80%** of ICP criteria (industry, size, role, geography)
- Show buying intent via engagement (open/click/reply) or behaviour signals
- Confirm interest and agree to a meeting time
- Have no obvious budget/authority mismatch

---

## 6.12 Client Delivery Standards

Each delivered lead includes:
- Name, title, company, contact details
- Why they were selected (ICP fit + engagement evidence)
- Meeting time + calendar invite link
- Notes on interactions/objections

Leads are delivered in batches to align with sales team capacity.

---

## 6.13 Feedback & Optimization Loop

Clients must provide feedback on lead quality + meeting outcomes within **48 hours**. This feedback trains the models and improves future campaigns. Without feedback, SLAs may be paused.

---

## 6.14 SLA & Guarantee Scope

**Guaranteed:**
- The system automates at least **80%** of appointment-setting activities.
- Meeting volume targets are set during onboarding.
- If targets are not met, AutomateSystems continues the campaign at no extra cost until targets are met.

**Not guaranteed:** closed deals or revenue outcomes.

---

## 6.15 Failure Handling & Escalation

**Early signals:** low lead volume, poor engagement, high no-show rates.

**Escalation actions:** revisit ICP/messaging, adjust data sources, allocate more agents, run a calibration call.

---

## 6.16 Capacity & Scaling Rules

- Launch campaigns incrementally to protect quality
- Confirm capacity before adding campaigns
- Scale agent pools based on volume; avoid over-commitment

---

## 6.17 Contractual Guardrails

- Minimum **3-month** commitment (supports model training)
- Cancellation requires **30 days** notice
- Data privacy compliance remains the client’s responsibility; misuse voids guarantees

---

## 6.18 Competitive Differentiation

Unlike appointment-setting agencies or outsourced SDR services:
- Uses AI agents for prospecting, qualification, and personalized outreach
- Pricing tied to lead volume rather than hourly work
- Includes a clear **80% cost reduction** guarantee in appointment-setting activities
- Emphasizes data privacy + model transparency
